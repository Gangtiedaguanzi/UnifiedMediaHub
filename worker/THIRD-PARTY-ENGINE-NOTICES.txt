Small Telegram stripped-thumbnail port:
worker/src/shared/telegram-thumbnails.mjs and telegram-jpeg-header.mjs use the
623-byte header/expansion from gotd/td v0.140.0 telegram/thumbnail/thumbnail.go:
https://github.com/gotd/td/blob/v0.140.0/telegram/thumbnail/thumbnail.go
The public 28-byte golden fixture is from the same version's thumbnail_test.go.
